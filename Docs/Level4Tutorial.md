# Level 4: coffee product commercial

The pre-acceptance chair-and-actor lesson remains unchanged: place a chair and actor from Elements, purchase the megaphone, practise cues/movement and sitting. Machine interaction remains optional discovery. Acceptance removes practice actors and chair; the player builds the commercial independently.

Contract 4's focus is a packaged-product overview followed by actor coffee use, not the old greeting/action/seated-break story or Level 3's soft-light/color recipe:

1. Choose Cafe Corner or Coffee Interior using CHOOSE SET. A plain backdrop is not enough. Place cup, Kape packaging and at least one actor; preserve this setup until footage ingestion.
2. Record Product Overview with cup and packaging fully visible. Record Coffee Use separately: the full actor and cup in the cafe, drinking in Action while holding coffee or Using Machine with coffee visible. Do not change evidence type or lose required subjects during a take. Saved visibility requires every sampled frame; a mixed/incomplete take must be re-recorded, not merely trimmed.
3. In the edit, Product Overview must precede Coffee Use. Join clips from 0s with no gaps/overlaps (0.05s alignment tolerance); every segment, including split pieces, must last at least 2s and remain inside source bounds. Duration is 30-45s inclusive.
4. No timeline overlays. Greeting, sitting, particular shot sizes, a particular light model and three-point lighting are not hard requirements. Music, transitions, set color and color grading remain creative choices.

`CoffeeStoryRules` owns duration/segment limits, required checklist, recording/editing guidance and validation diagnostics. `ContractUIManager` displays that shared guidance in both studio and editor [TAB] briefs, with a quick delivery summary, prominent mandatory checks and separate quality thresholds. `ContractGrader` explains missing overview/use evidence, wrong order, short segments, timing and overlays separately; the existing pass logic is unchanged. `CoffeeStoryEditLesson` teaches selection, playhead splits [B], undo [Ctrl + Z] and selected-clip color grading.

Contract agreements now separate Pre-Production (required cafe set and subjects), Production (original-take recording evidence), Post-Production (ordering, minimum segment lengths and 30-45s delivery), Budget and Payment, and approval/signature terms. The player must scroll to the bottom before ACCEPT & SIGN unlocks. A name/pen signing animation finishes before the job's existing acceptance callback runs. TAB reference viewing cannot sign or issue another advance. The Level 4 recording/editing guidance still comes directly from `CoffeeStoryRules`.

Earlier-level contracts share the new agreement layout; multiplayer-specific role/tutorial logic is not redesigned. No scenes, prefabs, product catalogs or set transforms are changed.
