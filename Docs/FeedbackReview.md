# Client feedback papers

The feedback view uses the existing exports from FEEDBACK.psd under `Assets/UI/FEEDBACK`: wood desk, five original paper layers, paperclip, stamping hand and APPROVED/FAILED stamps. Thirteen `feedback*` keys in `Resources/ExportUIArt.asset` reference the original textures, including the Boss poses. No original texture or PSD was overwritten. Boss sprite UV bounds omit the transparent margins of the original 1920x1080 portraits, without changing their imports or other Boss screens.

`FeedbackReport` is a presentation-only splitter of `ProductionGrades.feedback`: current numbered headings and legacy dashed headings map to Pre-Production, Production and Post-Production. The tracked BUDGET REVIEW maps to paper four; approval gates, next-step coaching and personal best map to Client Decision. All detailed notes remain available in scrollable regions. White/green/red/yellow notes use dark, readable parchment colors. Missing old budget data is identified, not fabricated. The final stamp follows the saved letter grade, never the overall number alone: an F remains FAILED even at 99/100.

`FeedbackPaperUI` adds each new paper with an unscaled-time slide/rotation. Back revisits the stack without changing scores. The final paper animates the original hand and stamp. Navigation is disabled during an entrance/stamp and the final action has an exactly-once guard. Long notes scroll independently on each paper. Left/Right arrows also navigate. Career results are paper-only: CLOSE is hidden and Escape cannot dismiss them to a blank or legacy screen; the decision paper supplies Continue/Replay. Crew reviews retain their existing close-to-studio behavior. Programmatic Hide still cancels animations cleanly for scene/room cleanup and the final action. Nothing in the view applies rewards or accepts a contract.

`GradeManager` retains the existing career reward guards, analytics completion and progression. It supplies ReturnToStudio/RetryContract to `FinalGradePanelUI` before displaying results. Results automatically open the first paper. `FinalGradePanelUI` hides its old summary/feedback children and disables only its own legacy Canvas/raycaster in Awake and DisplayResults; its controller and the separate paper Canvas stay active. The big-letter summary, FEEDBACK & BUDGET and old return buttons are no longer part of the visible flow. The legacy automatic failure popup no longer covers the paper review. Legacy serialized fields, public UnityEvent methods and scene/prefab GUIDs are preserved. The final action is REPLAY CONTRACT, CONTINUE TO LEVEL n or FINISH CAMPAIGN, as appropriate.

The first-commercial editor tutorial explains all five papers, Next/Back, scrolling and the final stamp. Boss portrait and dialogue paper are visible only for a submission made during the active first-commercial tutorial (`CrossSceneData.submittedWithTutorial`, captured before the lesson finishes), until the player completes the final paper (`FeedbackPaper.TutorialSeen`, career-local). Normal results, developer-bypassed tutorials, and crew reviews hide both the portrait and speech paper; even a first Level 1 submission does not automatically count as a tutorial. Reusing the review view reapplies visibility on every opening. Room reviews never persist that career flag. Tutorial Boss/task/highlighter screen-space root canvases share Scale With Screen Size. World-space equipment/shop surfaces are excluded.

The results own a screen-space overlay canvas and 1920x1080 reference frame. CanvasScaler uses Scale With Screen Size / Expand: all artwork and buttons keep their proportions and remain on screen, with margins at non-16:9 ratios. A full-canvas modal backdrop blocks underlying clicks even in those margins. This intentionally preserves the landscape paper design rather than reflowing it into a phone layout.

`FeedbackTypography` supplies bundled pen lettering: Kalam Regular for notes, scores, tutorial review speech and navigation, with existing Allura Regular for cursive headings. Kalam and its SIL Open Font License are included in `Resources/FeedbackFonts` (upstream: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/kalam); Allura remains in `Resources/ContractFonts`. Dynamic TMP atlases and the existing TMP fallback cover supported text without depending on fonts installed on the player's computer. Dark blue ink, subtle notebook rules, red margins, paper shadows and parchment button faces complement the original artwork. Normal reviews replace the tutorial Boss area with a five-department contents paper highlighting the current result. Notes stay a fixed readable size in a 1040-wide scroll area; only headings, counters and button labels auto-fit. Styling does not change grades, payments or the review actions.

Crew results use the same five papers, but show the shared opening/current budget with an explicit note that categorized room expenses are not tracked. Local page navigation sends no room commands. Only the host gets NEXT CONTRACT or RETRY CONTRACT; retry uses the existing Boss confirmation. Editors can revise their edit, and other crew members close the review. The final callback still goes through existing authority checks. Resubmitting from the editor displays a response even when the room remains in the review phase. Offline career funds and progress are untouched.

## Verification

`feedback-review-check.ps1` in the chat workspace compiles all runtime scripts against actual Unity Editor and standalone-player references, then editor tools, and runs isolated report/navigation checks. `feedback-layout-check.ps1` checks source navigation bounds, all 13 texture GUID references, bundled font data/licensing, Boss text fitting and eight approximate WPF renders for each of normal and tutorial mode using the original art and packaged Kalam/Allura fonts: all five papers, passed/failed, 1920x1080, 1280x720, 1366x768, 1024x768, 2560x1080 and 1080x1920. These are not live Unity/TMP/button/animation validation or a full build.
